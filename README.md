Week 2: Data Identification, Sourcing Strategy & Initial Analysis Planning

Project Title

Data-Driven Customer Purchase Prediction and Personalized Product Recommendations in the Indian Beauty & Wellness Industry

About the Project

This project focuses on understanding customer shopping behaviour in the Indian beauty and wellness industry. The main aim is to identify customer purchasing patterns, understand popular products, analyse repeat purchases, and explore how data science can help businesses provide better product recommendations.

In Week 2, I focused on identifying suitable datasets, exploring public data sources, planning data collection, and understanding how to prepare data for further analysis.

Objectives

Identify useful datasets for beauty and wellness analysis.

Understand customer purchasing behaviour.

Analyse popular products and product categories.

Study customer purchase frequency and spending patterns.

Explore repeat-purchase prediction within 60 days.

Plan personalized product recommendations.

Understand data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA).

Data Sources

The following public resources were identified:

Open Beauty Facts: https://world.openbeautyfacts.org/

Product names, brands, categories and ingredients.

UCI Online Retail Dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail

Transaction records, customer IDs, quantities and prices.

Government of India: https://microdata.gov.in/NADA/index.php/catalog/CEXP

Household consumption and spending information.

Google Trends: https://trends.google.com/trends/

Search interest in beauty-related products.

Meta Content Library: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-content-library/

Research into public discussions about beauty products, subject to access requirements.

Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets

Additional datasets for practice and analysis.
